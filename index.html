Privacy Policy

AgriPost Automator

Last updated: August 13, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how AgriPost Automator ("we," "our," or "the App") collects, uses, and protects information when you use our application, including its integration with Facebook and Meta services.

1. Information We Collect

When you connect AgriPost Automator to your Facebook account, we may collect the following information, depending on the permissions you grant:

  • Basic profile information: your name and public profile picture
  • Facebook Page information: names, IDs, and details of Pages you manage
  • Page content: posts, images, and text you create or schedule through the App
  • Page access tokens: used solely to publish content to your Page(s) on your behalf
  • Contact information: your email address, if provided during setup

We do not collect sensitive personal data such as health information, financial details, or precise location data.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Authenticate your account and connect to your Facebook Page(s)
  • Automate and schedule posts to your Facebook Page(s) on your behalf
  • Provide customer support and respond to inquiries
  • Improve and maintain the functionality of the App
  • Communicate important updates about the App

We do not use your data for advertising purposes, and we do not sell your data to third parties.

3. How We Share Your Information

We do not share, sell, or rent your personal information to third parties, except:

  • With Meta/Facebook, as necessary to provide the App's core functionality (e.g., publishing posts via the Facebook Graph API)
  • When required by law, such as in response to a valid legal request from authorities
  • To protect our rights, in cases involving fraud, security threats, or violations of our terms

4. Data Storage and Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. Access tokens and credentials are stored securely and are only used to perform actions you have explicitly authorized (such as posting to your Page).

5. Data Retention

We retain your information only for as long as necessary to provide the App's services or as required by law. If you disconnect AgriPost Automator from your Facebook account, we will delete your stored access tokens and associated data within a reasonable period.

6. Your Rights and Data Deletion

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Withdraw consent and disconnect the App from your Facebook account at any time

To request data deletion, please contact us at somrat99official@gmail.com, or use Facebook's built-in app removal feature under your Facebook Settings > Apps and Websites.

7. Children's Privacy

AgriPost Automator is not directed at individuals under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Third-Party Services

AgriPost Automator uses the Facebook Graph API to provide its services. Your use of Facebook is also governed by Facebook's Data Policy. We encourage you to review it.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
